This Cookie Notice explains how CronAPI ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies on [https://cronapi.hakinsight.com](https://cronapi.hakinsight.com) and the CronAPI dashboard.

---

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and maintain session state.

---

## Cookies We Use

CronAPI uses only **essential cookies** — cookies that are strictly necessary to provide the core functionality of the service.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `cronapi_session` | Stores your authenticated dashboard session token. Required to stay logged in to the dashboard. | 30 days (or until logout) |

We do **not** use:
- Analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics)
- Advertising or tracking cookies
- Third-party social media cookies
- Performance profiling cookies

---

## Essential Cookies Are Not Optional

Because we only use strictly essential cookies — and do not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking — we do not display a cookie consent banner. Essential cookies are exempt from consent requirements under GDPR and ePrivacy regulations as they are necessary for the service to function.

If you block all cookies in your browser, you will not be able to log in to the dashboard. The public-facing landing page and API do not require cookies.

---

## Third-Party Cookies

Our payment processor, **Stripe**, may set cookies when you interact with billing flows. These cookies are managed by Stripe and subject to [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). We do not control these cookies.

---

## Your Choices

- **Browser settings:** You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent you from using the dashboard.
- **Logout:** Logging out of the dashboard immediately invalidates your session cookie.

We will update this Cookie Notice if our cookie usage changes. Material changes will be communicated via the dashboard or email.
