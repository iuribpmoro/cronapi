Questions

Who can join the referral program?
Any CronAPI user with an existing account can get a referral link. Free and paid users are both eligible.
What counts as a successful referral?
When someone signs up using your referral link and upgrades to the Indie or Pro plan (or activates their free trial month), that counts as a successful referral.
Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?
No. Every successful referral adds another free month to your account. Refer 12 friends and get a year free.
What does the referred person get?
The first month of the Indie plan is free — that's $9 off, no credit card required for the trial month.
When is the credit applied to my account?
Credits are applied automatically when your referral activates. You'll receive an email confirmation.
Can credits be combined with other promotions?
Yes. Referral credits stack with each other and can be used alongside any other CronAPI promotion.
Can I refer myself or create fake accounts?
No. Referrals must come from genuinely new users. We check for duplicate emails, payment methods, and IP addresses. Abuse will result in account suspension.
Referral credits apply to the Indie plan ($9/mo). Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value. CronAPI reserves the right to modify or terminate the referral program with 30 days notice. Questions? hello@cronapi.hakinsight.com
