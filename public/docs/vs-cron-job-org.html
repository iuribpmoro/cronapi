CronAPI vs cron-job.org

cron-job.org is one of the most popular free cron services. It has an extremely generous free tier and is community-funded. CronAPI is a paid service designed for developers who need stronger reliability guarantees, request signing, and API-first job management. Here's how they compare.

cron-job.org wins on price — especially the free tier, which offers unlimited jobs with per-minute scheduling at no cost. If price is your primary concern, cron-job.org is the clear choice.

Where CronAPI Wins

  • HMAC signing. Every request carries a cryptographic signature your endpoint can verify. cron-job.org sends unauthenticated requests — your endpoint can't distinguish a legitimate scheduler call from an attacker.
  • Reliable retries. CronAPI automatically retries failed executions with exponential backoff. cron-job.org has limited retry support.
  • API-first. CronAPI is designed to be controlled programmatically — register, update, and delete jobs from your deployment scripts without touching a dashboard.
  • Execution ID. CronAPI includes a unique execution ID in every request header, making idempotent endpoint design straightforward.

Where cron-job.org Wins

  • Free tier. Unlimited jobs with per-minute scheduling at zero cost. Unmatched for side projects and development environments.
  • Price at scale. For high job counts, cron-job.org is significantly cheaper.
  • Established service. cron-job.org has been running for many years with a well-known reliability track record.

When to Choose Each

Choose cron-job.org if: you're building a side project, want free per-minute scheduling, don't need HMAC security, and are comfortable with a dashboard-first workflow.

Choose CronAPI if: you're building a production service that needs request authentication, automatic retries, and programmatic job management. If your endpoint verifies signatures before processing — which it should — cron-job.org's lack of HMAC signing is a blocker.
